# Privacy policy

**Mac Mode for WhatsApp Web** collects, stores and transmits no data.

Effective date: 10 October 2026.

## What the extension does

- It runs only on `https://web.whatsapp.com/`.
- It adds styles to the page and listens for its own keyboard shortcuts.
- It reads the page's structure to check that its styles and shortcuts still fit. The result stays in the page and only shows or hides a small status dot.

## What the extension does not do

- It does not collect, store or send your messages, contacts or any other data.
- It makes no network requests and loads no remote code.
- It uses no extension storage, cookies or analytics.
- It requests no Chrome permissions beyond running on `web.whatsapp.com`.
- It does not send messages or change anything in your WhatsApp account.

## Karabiner-Elements rule

The optional Karabiner-Elements rule in this repository remaps keys on your Mac only. It sends nothing anywhere.

## Changes

Any change to this policy will appear in this file, with a new effective date.
